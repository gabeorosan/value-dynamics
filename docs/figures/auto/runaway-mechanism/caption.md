# Why the rare runaway seeds run away: sustained beyond-chance judge selection

Each round of the selection loop the model writes 6 answers per question, a judge keeps 2, and the model fine-tunes on the kept answers; pool = mean candidate risk of the 6-answer pool, selection gap = mean risk of the kept answers minus the pool mean, and the null percentile is the observed gap's rank among 4,000 random 2-of-6 keeps of the same pool. **Panel A** is the four-judge natural experiment on the OLMo risk model (K2 chassis, seed 2): all four judge conditions start from the identical round-0 pool (0.38), and only the frozen neutral judge runs away (0.38 → 0.61, riding gaps of +0.13, +0.01, +0.25) while random keep-2 on the same pools decays to 0.21 — early luck without a judge's taste does not run away. **Panel B** plots all 51 round-to-round transitions of the 17 OLMo K2 runs: pool drift tracks the same-round gap (drift ≈ 1.02 × gap, r = 0.82, fit through the 27 neutral-judge and self-judge rounds), rounds with |gap| < 0.05 drift −0.05 to +0.01 on average (no momentum, no self-sustaining "polluted pool"), and the two runaway runs are the only runs with two rounds each whose gap beat ≥ 90% of the random-keep null (red rings). **Panel C** contrasts mechanisms per run: the OLMo runaways bank large summed gaps (+0.38 → +0.49 move, +0.39 → +0.24), while the Qwen K1 self-judge fan moves its forced-choice behavioral endpoint by −0.33 to +0.42 with summed gaps of −0.22 to +0.06 — movement without selection, i.e. training instability rather than judge taste. Channels differ by design in panel C: OLMo movement is the generated-pool move (the gap's own channel); Qwen movement is the behavioral endpoint move, because its generated pools barely move while behavior fans by 0.75.

Source data: `experiments/runaway_decomposition.json` (per-round pools, gaps, null percentiles, drifts; per-run summed gaps and total drifts; pooled slopes and zero-gap drifts) and `experiments/kaggle/kaggle_k1_qwen_anchor_grid/output/k1_qwen_anchor.json` (Qwen behavioral trajectories).
