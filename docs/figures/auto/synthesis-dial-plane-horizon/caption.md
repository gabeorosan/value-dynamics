# synthesis-dial-plane-horizon

**The background shows the endpoint model's predicted four-round value change;
each dot shows observed change.** The figure contains 32 self-only runs, with
all training candidates generated by the organism. Runs are placed by
round-one agreement and spread. Because the two
plotted axes do not include the starting state, the background averages over
the plotted runs' starting states:
`mean_i[clip(q1_i + 4ρσ, 0, 1) − v1_i]`, where `q1` is the run's initial
own-candidate mean and `v1` is its initial measured value.
This is the parameter-free recurrence behind the headline endpoint result: it
holds round-one `ρ` and `σ` fixed. A separate fitted recurrence predicts how
spread changes, but feeding that trajectory back into the rollout worsens
endpoint MAE (0.139 versus 0.127 with round-one spread held fixed), so that
variant is not used in this figure.
The labeled bands mark predicted changes of −0.4, −0.2, 0, +0.2, and +0.4.
Dot shape identifies the model family and value axis, and dots and background
use the same value-change scale.

The plot contains 32 modelable self-only four-round runs: 8 moved up, 11 moved
down, and 13 remained within 0.15 of their round-one value. Mixed-pool runs are
excluded because their movement also includes the shift in candidate supply.
Seven self-only four-round runs with undefined round-one agreement and 11
eight-round judge-schedule runs are also excluded.

On these 32 runs, endpoint MAE is 0.159 versus 0.269 for assuming no change; the
model predicts the direction of 16 of the 19 runs that move by at least 0.15.

Sources: `experiments/spread_rollout_bakeoff.json` and
`experiments/selection_response_predictor.json`. Regenerate with
`python3 synthesis-dial-plane-horizon.py` from this directory.
